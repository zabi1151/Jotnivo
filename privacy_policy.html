Privacy Policy for Jotnivo

Application: Jotnivo — Daily Organizer (com.zabidev.jotnivo)
Developer / Owner: Zohaib / Zabi.dev
Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Jotnivo ("we", "our", or "us") is built as a local-first personal organizer for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Jotnivo mobile application.

1. Local-First Data Storage & Your Content

Jotnivo does not require you to create an account or sign in. All personal content that you create, save, or import into Jotnivo is stored strictly locally on your device inside Android app-private storage, including:

  • Notes, text snippets, and titles
  • Saved web links (URLs) and domains
  • Checklists and task items
  • Imported images and screenshots saved from your gallery or the Android Share sheet
  • Collections, tags, pin/archive/trash states, and scheduled reminder times

We do not operate any cloud server that collects, reads, or syncs your personal notes, checklists, links, or images. Automatic Android OS cloud auto-backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false") so your private organizer database is never silently uploaded to third-party cloud storage without your knowledge.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of Jotnivo, the app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display Banner, Native Advanced, and Interstitial advertisements.

Google AdMob, as a third-party ad network, may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information to provide, personalize, and measure advertisements, as well as to detect fraud and abuse. This information may include:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID
  • Network & Location Information: IP address and coarse location derived from your IP address
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad views, clicks, app launch events, device model, OS version, and crash/performance diagnostics related to ad rendering

Google AdMob does not have access to the private notes, checklists, links, or screenshots stored inside your local Jotnivo database.

You can manage or reset your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time on your Android device by going to Settings > Google > All services > Ads. To learn more about how Google collects and uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. App Permissions We Request & Why

Jotnivo follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the Android permissions required for core app features:

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to load advertisements via Google AdMob and allow you to open saved external links in your browser.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Requested on Android 13+ so Jotnivo can display local reminder notifications when your scheduled items become due. By default, sensitive item titles can be concealed on your lock screen via Jotnivo Settings.
  • Exact Alarms & Boot Completed (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to trigger your scheduled reminders on time and automatically restore active reminder schedules after your phone restarts, updates, or changes timezones.
  • Android Photo Picker & Share Intent: When you attach an image or share content into Jotnivo, the app only accesses the specific media file or text you explicitly select. Jotnivo never requests broad background access to your entire photo library.

4. Local Backup & Restore (ZIP Archives)

Jotnivo includes a manual Local Backup & Restore tool that exports your database and attachments into a standard .zip archive at a file location you choose via the Android system file picker.

  • Exported ZIP backup files contain your notes and attachments in unencrypted form so you can inspect or restore them across devices.
  • Once exported outside Jotnivo's private storage, the security of that backup file depends on where you store or share it. Please keep exported backup archives in a secure, trusted location.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your organizer data resides locally on your device, you have complete control over its retention and deletion:

  • You can move items to Trash and permanently delete individual items or tap Empty Trash at any time inside Jotnivo Settings.
  • Permanently deleting an item immediately removes its database record and deletes its associated image files from app-private storage.
  • Uninstalling Jotnivo or clearing app data in Android System Settings permanently erases all locally stored data on your device (except any backup ZIP files you manually exported to external folders).

6. Children's Privacy

Jotnivo is a general-audience productivity utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we introduce new features in future releases. Any updates will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
